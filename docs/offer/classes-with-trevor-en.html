About me

I've been a language teacher for 13+ years. Today I teach private English and Portuguese classes online, one-on-one, with 200+ students so far.

I'm from Arkansas, USA. I earned my degree in Spanish at the University of the Ozarks and taught Spanish at the University of Missouri, where I took my first Portuguese classes. In 2020 I went to Brazil with the Fulbright program to teach English at the Federal University of Pará, then stayed three years teaching at English schools and a bilingual school.

Private classes

Every class is one hour, one-on-one, on Google Meet, at a time you pick in the schedule app. Times show in your own time zone; I teach from Denver (Mountain Time).

English classes are taught 100% in English. Portuguese classes start in English and bridge into Portuguese conversation.

Conversation firstYou talk from day one. Grammar comes in as you need it, and the words stick because you have used them.
Once or twice a weekPick the times that work for you. A calendar invite with your Meet link arrives right away.

Built around your goalsTravel, work, exams, or just confidence: we work on what you need, with conversation, grammar, videos, music, games and homework as it fits.

Cancellation policy

Every package comes with one free cancellation, no questions asked. After that, the 24-hour rule applies.

More than 24 hours aheadCancel or move a class in the app as often as you need. It never counts.
Less than 24 hours ahead, or a missed classThe first time in a package is free: the class doesn't count and we find another time. After that, it counts as one of your classes.
If I cancel a classIt never counts, and you book another time.
Good to knowA class requested less than 72 hours ahead stays pending until I approve it. Packages aren't refunded; you use one up by taking the classes you paid for.
