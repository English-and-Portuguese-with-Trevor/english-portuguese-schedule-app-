## The ribbon style, readability first
The landing repo's ribbon style (its home and About pages): the sites' palette as the theme tokens in `globals.css` (cream ground, ink text, forest buttons, gold for the one confirming button), Inter for text, DM Serif Display for titles (`.title`), glass cards (`.glass`, the `Card` default), pill buttons. The ribbons (`src/components/ribbons.tsx`, the landing repo's drawing) go only behind the login and status cards and the time picker panel, faint (`.stage-soft`). Readability over decoration: a notch bigger base size, no text on busy backgrounds, contrast kept. The admin pages share the tokens and nothing more.

## 30-minute classes for new students
Until Trevor marks someone as his student (lesson access `granted` or `lifetime`, or a class package) they book only 30-minute classes inside his availability windows; his students book the windows' length (1 hour). `isClassStudent` / `NEW_STUDENT_CLASS_MINUTES` in `src/lib/types.ts` pick the times on the dashboard; `private.class_minutes` (used by `assert_lesson_time` for bookings and reschedules) enforces it in the database. Keep the two in step. Trevor's own bookings aren't limited. A student may create at most 8 bookings in 24 hours, cancelled ones included (`assert_booking_limits`, `20261010030000_booking_change_limit.sql`), so a book-and-cancel loop can't flood Trevor's phone and the day's Gmail quota.

Keep the two in step. Trevor edits them on `/admin/availability` (Days off) and the landing dashboard's Availability screen. Classes already booked on a day off stay booked.

## My notes (students)
Private students (a `class_package` on the profile), admins and accounts with `profiles.notes_access` have notes on the notebook site, `/notebook/` (the `notebook` repo, with My progress; "My notes" in every account menu, right under My progress; shown only to those accounts): topics with sub-notes at any depth, tags (used ones suggested), a search box and a tag menu; saved as they type. `public.student_notes` (`supabase/migrations/20261005220000_student_notes.sql`, `parent_id` for sub-notes): the student reads and writes their own. Sharing (`20261008160000_shared_notes.sql`): a note is private until the student ticks "Share with Trevor" (`private` false; a sub-note of a shared note starts shared); admins read and edit shared notes, never add, delete, move or make one private (the `student_note_changed` trigger); both type at once: the text is a Yjs document (`ydoc`, beside the plain `body`) kept in step over the note's private Realtime channel `note:<id>`, which only those who can read the note may join (`realtime.messages` policies). Trevor opens a student's notes from the admin dashboard's Student page (Notes, the notebook site's `#/s/<id>`). The privacy page says so; keep it in step. Only those accounts can start a note (`20261006000000_notes_for_private_students.sql`, `20261006020000_notes_access.sql`; `notes_access` is not a class package: no class emails or hour-long classes, and new accounts start without it); anyone keeps reading, changing and deleting the notes they have, so a student whose package ends keeps them until the cleanup. Kept a year from the last use: opening the notes (`touch_my_notes`) or changing a note sets `profiles.notes_active_at`; after 11 months unused the daily job sends `emails.notesExpiring` once (`cleanUpNotes`, `src/lib/notes-cleanup.ts`; `claim_notes_warnings` marks `notes_warned_at` first, a failed email is handed back), in the student's site language; `delete_stale_notes` deletes them a year after the last use and at least 30 days after that email. While Gmail isn't set up nothing is warned or deleted.

## Admin alerts (new sign-ups, new subscribers)
- Database triggers fill `admin_alerts` (a new `profiles` row; a `billing` subscription that starts), as does `flag_my_class`: students with lesson access (granted, subscriber, lifetime) flag each class once with a set reason, no notes, and take it back (`unflag_my_class` deletes its alert). Flags are pushed after a one-minute wait (pg_cron `push-waiting-flags`; a flag taken back sooner is never pushed) and emailed only in the 6:30 AM Mountain summary (`/api/cron/flags`). The lessons and activities sites' "Report an issue" flags make `report` alerts the same way (`report_issue` / `unreport_issue`, `content_reports`). The admin header's bell shows the unread count; `/admin/alerts` lists them. Push is turned on only from the admin dashboard (landing repo, `/admin/#/alerts`, service worker `public/admin/sw.js` there), one subscription per device; this app has no push switch or service worker and must not get one back (two switches meant two pushes).
- Class requests: a student's booking or reschedule left `PENDING` makes a `request` alert (trigger on `bookings`, `20261008200000_class_request_alerts.sql`), pushed after the same one-minute wait as flags (a request taken straight back is never pushed) and marked emailed when made, since "Approval needed" / "Reschedule requested" already email him.
- Push and email are marked separately: `pushed_at` (pushes, 2-day window) and `emailed_at` (sign-up and subscriber emails, 14-day window; flags and reports use it for the morning email), both claimed in `claim_alert_pushes`, so a retried email never pushes again.
- Every alert is emailed to `ADMIN_NOTIFY_EMAIL` and pushed: DB (pg_net) -> `/api/alerts/push` (Bearer `CRON_SECRET`) -> `emails.adminAlerts` + `web-push` (`src/lib/admin-push.ts`); the daily job sends any the DB call missed. The job routes call the database with the service role key (`SUPABASE_SERVICE_ROLE_KEY` in Vercel, `createServerJobClient`); the secret-guarded functions and `admin_timezone` are executable by `service_role` only, with `check_cron_secret` as the second lock (`20261010040000_job_functions_service_role.sql`). The VAPID keys live in `private.app_settings`; never replace them. See the README's "Alerts" section. Admin-only, so English only.

## Daily practice reminder (students)
Students with lesson access get one push a day for the activities site's daily practice (`#/daily` there, its `src/lib/daily.js`). `supabase/migrations/20261008120000_daily_practice.sql`: `daily_practice` (lessons, size 5 to 25, `push_hour` in the student's `profiles.timezone`, `done_on`, `pushed_on`; the student and admins set it) and `practice_push_subscriptions` (the students' devices, apart from the admin's `push_subscriptions` so an admin alert never reaches a student; same VAPID keys). pg_cron `practice-pushes` (3 minutes past every hour) calls `/api/practice/push` (Bearer `CRON_SECRET`), which sends `claim_practice_pushes`' devices "Today's practice" in the student's site language (`src/lib/practice-push.ts`); claiming marks `pushed_on`, so once a day, and never once that day's set is done. The service worker is the activities site's; this app has none. A push, not an email (see Email volume).

## The admin dashboard calls this app
